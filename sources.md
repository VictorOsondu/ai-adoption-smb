# Sources and Verification Notes

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24.

This playbook is practical SMB guidance, not legal, data-protection, insurance, employment, security, or regulatory advice. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, sector, client contract, tool, account type, data class, and use case.

## AI Literacy and Governance

- [European Commission: AI literacy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-talent-skills-and-literacy) - Article 4 of the EU AI Act entered into application on 2025-02-02 and requires providers and deployers to ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy for staff and other people dealing with AI systems on their behalf.
- [European Commission: AI literacy Q&A](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/ai-literacy-questions-answers) - explains Article 4 scope, context, affected persons, enforcement, and the Commission's approach. The Commission notes that listed practices do not automatically create a presumption of compliance.
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) - voluntary framework for managing AI risks to individuals, organisations, and society.
- [NIST AI Resource Center](https://airc.nist.gov/) - operational resources for applying the AI RMF.

## Data Handling and Tool Tiers

- [OpenAI: Business data privacy, security, and compliance](https://openai.com/business-data/) - describes business-data commitments, training defaults for business products, encryption, retention controls, security, DPAs, and data residency.
- [OpenAI: How your data is used to improve model performance](https://openai.com/policies/how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance/) - explains differences between individual services and business products for model-improvement data use.
- [OpenAI Help: Managing data, sharing, and privacy in ChatGPT Business](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8798634-managing-data-sharing-and-privacy-in-chatgpt-business) - plan-specific guidance on workspace data, sharing, admin visibility, training, and export limitations.

## Claims to Re-check Often

- EU AI Act Article 4 implementation, enforcement guidance, and national market-surveillance authority practice.
- Whether a vendor uses prompts, outputs, files, feedback, telemetry, logs, or connected-app data for training or product improvement.
- Retention, deletion, human-review, memory, connected-app, export, and admin controls for the exact plan purchased.
- Client, insurer, sector, employment, data-protection, and contractual requirements for AI use.
- Whether a tool is approved for customer, employee, client, financial, health, confidential, regulated, or proprietary data.
