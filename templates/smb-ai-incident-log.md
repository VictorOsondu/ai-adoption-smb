# SMB AI Incident Log

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24. See [sources and verification notes](../sources.md).

Use this for AI incidents and near misses. Keep sensitive details out of shared copies; link to restricted records where needed.

## Incident Types

- Confidential, client, customer, employee, personal, financial, health, safeguarding, or restricted data entered into the wrong tool.
- Credentials, API keys, tokens, private links, or secrets entered into AI.
- AI-assisted output sent externally with wrong facts, prices, promises, or advice.
- AI used to make or shape a decision about a person.
- Prompt injection or chatbot misuse.
- Unapproved tool used for work.
- Vendor breach, outage, terms change, or data-handling change.

## Immediate Actions

- Stop the unsafe use.
- Delete the chat, upload, link, or memory where possible.
- Rotate or revoke exposed credentials and secrets.
- Tell the owner, manager, DPO/data lead, IT/security, legal, insurer, or client contact where relevant.
- Record what happened, when, with which tool, and what was done.
- Update ground rules, training, tool settings, or the tool register.
