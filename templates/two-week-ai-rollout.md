# Two-Week SMB AI Rollout

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24. See [sources and verification notes](../sources.md).

Use this to move from scattered AI use to a simple, governed start.

## Week 1: Ground Rules and One Safe Win

### Day 1: Owner Decision

- Name the AI owner.
- Choose the first approved tool or confirm existing tool.
- Decide which data classes are off-limits.
- Share the [AI Ground Rules starter](ai-ground-rules-starter.md).

### Day 2: Tool Register

- Add current tools to the [SMB AI tool register](smb-ai-tool-register.md).
- Block or pause tools used for sensitive data without review.
- Confirm public vs approved work tool rules.

### Day 3: Pick One Improve Task

Choose one low-risk recurring task:

- meeting notes to actions
- internal SOP draft
- public research summary
- first-draft newsletter
- non-sensitive FAQ
- internal checklist

Avoid first wins that involve client data, HR decisions, customer commitments, credentials, or regulated data.

### Days 4-5: Test and Document

- Try the task with 2-3 users.
- Record the prompt pattern.
- Record the human checks.
- Note what failed.

## Week 2: Train, Review, and Decide

### Day 6: Staff Briefing

Cover:

- allowed uses
- public vs approved tools
- what never goes in
- checking outputs
- customer-facing review
- incident reporting

### Days 7-8: Run the Workflow

- Use the chosen Improve workflow on real low-risk work.
- Keep a short record of time saved and checks needed.
- Capture examples without sensitive data.

### Day 9: Review

Ask:

- Did it save time?
- Did output quality improve or decline?
- What risks appeared?
- What rule was unclear?
- What needs a better template?

### Day 10: Decide Next Step

Choose one:

- continue as approved
- continue with limits
- stop
- add a second Improve workflow
- begin a tool/vendor review
- move to a fuller policy and training record
