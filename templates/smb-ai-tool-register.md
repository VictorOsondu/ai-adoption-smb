# SMB AI Tool Register

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24. See [sources and verification notes](../sources.md).

Use this to track which AI tools are approved, for what, and under which limits. If a tool is not listed here, treat it as unapproved for customer, client, employee, financial, confidential, or regulated data.

## Data Classes

- **Public:** safe to publish.
- **Internal:** non-public business information that is low sensitivity.
- **Confidential:** client, customer, financial, proprietary, contract, strategy, or commercially sensitive information.
- **Personal:** information about identifiable people.
- **Restricted:** credentials, health, safeguarding, HR cases, payroll, legal disputes, regulated data, or high-risk client data.

## Review Questions

- Is the tool still needed?
- Are users still active and appropriate?
- Have settings, terms, retention, training, or connected apps changed?
- Has the vendor added new model providers, plugins, agents, browser access, voice, files, or memory?
- Has the tool been used outside its approved purpose?
- Are any incidents or near misses linked to it?
