## Minimum Checks

- [ ] We know whether prompts, outputs, files, feedback, telemetry, or logs are used for model training or product improvement.
- [ ] Retention and deletion are clear enough for the data we plan to use.
- [ ] Human-review rules are clear.
- [ ] Sub-processors and third-party model providers are named or acceptable for the risk.
- [ ] Connected apps, plugins, browser access, files, voice, screen, memory, and agents are understood.
- [ ] Admin controls, user offboarding, access control, and sharing settings are good enough for our size and risk.
- [ ] A DPA or equivalent terms are in place where personal data is involved.
- [ ] Breach or security-notification commitments are acceptable.
- [ ] Export and exit options are understood.
- [ ] The tool is affordable at expected use, not just during a trial.

## Decision

| Decision | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Approved | Use allowed for the listed use cases and data classes. |
| Approved with limits | Use allowed only under stated conditions. |
| Pilot | Testing only; no confidential, personal, restricted, client, customer, or employee data unless explicitly approved. |
| Blocked | Not suitable for work use. |
