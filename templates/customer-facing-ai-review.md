## Review

- [ ] A person has read the full output.
- [ ] Names, dates, prices, discounts, commitments, claims, citations, and numbers are verified.
- [ ] The output does not reveal client, customer, employee, financial, confidential, or internal information by accident.
- [ ] The output does not make unauthorised refunds, discounts, prices, promises, policy exceptions, eligibility decisions, or legal/financial advice.
- [ ] Tone, brand, accessibility, and plain-English quality are acceptable.
- [ ] Specialist review has happened where legal, financial, HR, health, safety, or compliance claims are involved.
- [ ] Any required disclosure or record is included.
