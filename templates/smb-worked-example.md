# SMB Worked Example

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24. See [sources and verification notes](../sources.md).

This is a synthetic example showing how a small business might use the templates. It is not a real client case.

## Business

**Northside Studio** is a 24-person design and marketing agency. Staff already use AI informally for copy drafts and research. The owner is worried about client data, inconsistent quality, and whether a client might ask about AI use.

## Stage Diagnosis

- Owner: Integrator.
- Team: Dabbler.
- Gap: staff are using AI, but rules and review are inconsistent.

## First Decision

The owner names the operations manager as AI owner and adopts the [AI Ground Rules starter](ai-ground-rules-starter.md).

## Tool Register

Initial register:

| Tool | Owner | Approved uses | Allowed data | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business AI workspace | Ops manager | Internal drafts, public research, non-sensitive content | Public, Internal | Approved |
| Personal free chatbots | Individual staff | Personal learning, public information only | Public | Allowed with limits |
| Browser AI extensions | None | Not reviewed | None | Blocked until reviewed |

## First Improve Workflow

Chosen workflow: turn internal meeting notes into action lists.

Rules:

- no client confidential information
- no employee personal data
- human checks actions before sharing
- action owner confirms accuracy

## First Risk Found

A team member had been pasting client briefs into a personal AI account to generate campaign ideas.

Action:

- stop the practice
- remind team of public-tool rules
- add client-brief handling to training
- review whether approved business tools can be used under contract and settings

## Outcome After Two Weeks

- Meeting-action workflow kept.
- Client-brief AI use paused until tool review.
- Browser extensions blocked until reviewed.
- Customer-facing content now uses the [Customer-facing AI review](customer-facing-ai-review.md).

## Lesson

The first win was not a dramatic automation. It was making existing use visible, safer, and repeatable.
